About NovaForge Digital Solutions GmbH

NovaForge is a mid-sized German technology company specializing in enterprise SaaS and digital transformation programs for regulated industries in DACH and Western Europe. We deliver large-scale platforms for education, ERP, and property management with strong compliance and quality requirements.
Position Overview

We are seeking an experienced Senior Program Manager to lead complex, multi-year Agile programs involving cross-functional teams of 30+ people. The role requires deep expertise in enterprise delivery frameworks, strong German-language stakeholder management, and proven experience in regulated environments.
Key Responsibilities

Lead large-scale Agile programs (SAFe) with budgets exceeding €5 million and teams of 30–50 people across multiple workstreams.
Act as Program Manager / Release Train Engineer, ensuring alignment between business, architecture, and delivery teams.
Drive end-to-end delivery of enterprise SaaS and ERP platforms with strict compliance (GDPR, ISO, industry-specific regulations).
Manage risks, dependencies, and financial tracking across multi-year roadmaps.
Coach Scrum Masters and Product Owners, implement SAFe practices, and report to C-level stakeholders in German.
Oversee CI/CD, cloud infrastructure (AWS), and quality gates in regulated environments.
Build strong relationships with German enterprise clients and internal stakeholders.

Required Qualifications & Experience

Minimum 8 years of full-time professional experience as Program Manager or Senior Project Manager in corporate / enterprise IT environments.

Hands-on experience with SAFe (Scaled Agile Framework) as Program Manager or RTE.
Strong experience with cloud platforms, especially AWS, and enterprise CI/CD pipelines.
Fluent German (B1 level minimum) – required for daily stakeholder communication, reporting, and client meetings. English is also required.
Solid background in regulated industries (finance, healthcare, public sector, or similar) is highly preferred.
Experience managing permanent full-time teams in Germany.

Mandatory Certifications

CSPO (Certified Scrum Product Owner) or equivalent advanced Scrum certification
Cloud Practitioner
ITIL Foundation (or higher)
